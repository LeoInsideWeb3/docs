A-Book Market-Maker Top-Up
one-direction fund loop

v1.0 · 2026-10-28 · NeoDAX team · Status: review

When the Binance market-making account runs low on available balance or margin risk rises, the system automatically transfers USDT from a dedicated treasury wallet to the approved Binance deposit address. After Binance credits the deposit, the MM program verifies it and moves funds into the Futures wallet. No human intervention on the happy path — every step auditable, recoverable, and pausable.

Objective

Objective & Fund Direction

01

One supported fund direction: from the Cage market-making treasury wallet, through the Binance Spot wallet, into the USDⓈ-M Futures wallet. No reverse flow.

ALARM

Not in scope:withdrawing from Binance, sweeping excess funds back, reusing customer deposit/withdrawal/balance flows, or auto-changing Binance API-key permissions.

The only fund direction
Cage treasury wallet (on-chain USDT)
    → Binance deposit address (fixed, managed by Cage allowlist)
    → MM Spot wallet (credited by Binance on arrival)
    → MM USDⓈ-M Futures wallet (internal transfer initiated by MM program)
NOTE

Separation of trust:Binance API keys live only in the MM program. mm_connector does not read Binance wallets, store Binance credentials, or call Binance private APIs. It only consumes MM-program-signed account snapshots.

Policy

Top-Up Policy & Amount Calculation

04

The policy answers one question: how much should we top up right now?The answer is the max of three deficit dimensions, minus in-flight amounts.

Amount formula
// A = available balance;  M = margin balance;  MM = maintenance margin
// F = forecast demand + reserve;  P = in-flight amount
// T = target available balance;  R = target margin ratio

available_deficit = max(0, T - A)
ratio_deficit     = max(0, MM / R - M)
forecast_deficit  = max(0, F - A)
gross_amount      = max(available_deficit, ratio_deficit, forecast_deficit)
requested_amount  = max(0, gross_amount - P)

The result is checked against asset precision, minimum amount, per-request limit, daily limit, Cage wallet balance, and Gas reserve.

NOTE

Why not just look at balance:margin ratio is an earlier risk indicator than balance — balance can still be adequate while maintenance-margin percentage is already critical. Taking the max across three dimensions covers the "balance is fine but risk has elevated" case.

WARN

Preventing duplicate top-ups:only one active request per venue_account_id + asset. In-flight amount P must be included in later evaluations — chain confirmation and Binance crediting each take minutes to hours, and ignoring in-flight would fire multiple transfers for the same deficit.

Flow

End-to-End Flow (10 steps)

05
STEP 1–2 OK

Read snapshot and evaluate policy

+

mm_connector periodically calls MM's GetTreasuryAccount, obtaining a source-timestamped, versioned, health-flagged account snapshot. On policy match, it writes the request and Outbox record in one transaction (reusing mm_connector's existing Inbox/Outbox/Worker Lease patterns).

Invalid snapshots (stale, version regression, wrong account/asset, invalid amounts, source_healthy=false, inconsistent margin ratio) → rejected, treated as no valid data.
STEP 3 WARN

Over limit → manual approval

+

If the amount exceeds the automatic limit, the request enters APPROVAL_PENDING. No on-chain action until approved.

STEP 4–5 OK

Send command to Cage; Cage validates and reserves limits

+

mm_connector sends CommandTreasuryTransferRequested with only the configured destination_id. Cage resolves the fixed address, network, and optional tag/memo from its allowlist, validates the target is enabled and matches, then atomically reserves the daily limit.

A separate command, not a reused customer-withdrawal event. Address rotation requires a new Cage version, dual review, and a small verification transfer before activation.
STEP 6–7 OK

Cage broadcasts and confirms on-chain

+

Cage sends the on-chain transaction, tracks confirmation depth, and publishes EventTreasuryTransferConfirmed. After confirmation, mm_connector calls the MM program's ObserveTopUp.

Once broadcast, the on-chain transaction cannot be cancelled. Pausing only stops subsequent automatic actions; already-broadcast transactions continue to reconcile.
STEP 8 OK

MM verifies Binance credit and transfers

+

The MM program queries Binance deposit history by asset, network, amount, address, and tx hash, confirms the Spot wallet was credited, then executes the Spot-to-Futures internal transfer, storing the Binance deposit ID and transfer ID.

Same idempotency key returns the original operation; never double-transfers. Multi-replica: shared idempotency or leader-elected execution.
STEP 9–10 OK

Final verification and completion

+

mm_connector obtains a snapshot newer than the internal transfer, confirms the Futures account increase and complete evidence. Only then does the request enter COMPLETED.

Completion = Cage event evidence + MM operation evidence + new snapshot showing the increase. Missing any one → not complete.

Risk

Risk Integration

08

The top-up workflow never modifies observed financial values. Risk can only recover after a fresh MM account snapshot.

StateMeaningTrigger
WARNINGNormal trading continuesWarning threshold reached (early top-up in progress)
REDUCE_ONLYReject orders that increase exposureStale MM data, or critical margin ratio reached
HALTEDReject A-Book orders (per existing PM Perp logic)Cage terminal failure with critical deficit; or funds in Spot but stuck
NOTE

Recovery condition:several consecutive healthy snapshots after completion, then step down gradually (HALTED → REDUCE_ONLY → WARNING → normal). Never simply re-enable because one top-up finished.

Rollout

Rollout (6 phases)

09
Phase 1 OK

Observation (no transfers)

+

Extend the MM account API, record snapshots and calculations. Verify data quality; no fund actions.

Phase 2 OK

Shadow policy

+

Run the policy and generate alerts; compare against operator judgment to calibrate thresholds. Iterate if shadow decisions diverge from experienced operators.

Phase 3 OK

Cage path (test network)

+

Deploy the dedicated wallet, allowlist, limits, and transfer contract; verify end-to-end on a test network.

Phase 4 WARN

Manual approval (all requests)

+

Every production top-up requires approval while validating the state machine and reconciliation. This is the longest phase — building confidence through real operations.

Phase 5 WARN

Limited automation

+

Enable small amounts, one asset, one network, low daily limit. Observe failure rate and manual-review frequency.

Phase 6 OK

Controlled expansion

+

Gradually adjust limits based on failure rates, latency, and manual-review records. Every phase retains a kill switch.

ALARM

9 items required before implementation:Binance account type & collateral asset · chain and token contract · fixed deposit address verified and registered in Cage · MM deposit-history and transfer endpoints · funding source, approvers, and daily limit · warning/critical/halt thresholds · automatic approval amount and manual SLA · deadlines for chain/Binance/internal transfer · MM multi-replica idempotency scheme.Production automatic transfers remain disabled until all items are confirmed.
